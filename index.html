What it does

Streak keeper

At 00.00 Reelo opens TikTok and sends one of your own messages to every friend you have a streak with. Phone locked? It waits until you unlock. Something failed? It tries again after 10 and 20 minutes, and reminds you at 20.00 and 23.00.

Reel counter

Counts new reels, not swipes. Each reel gets a fingerprint, so scrolling back up never counts twice. Optional daily limits, a Pause Card, night curfew and a 25-minute focus mode.

Home screen widgets

2×2, 4×2 and a 4×1 cinema marquee. See which streaks are still at risk, and tap the widget to send them right away.

Private by design

The app has no internet permission, so your data never leaves your phone. Text on screen is turned into a hash and thrown away.

Install in 4 steps

  1. Tap Download APK above.
  2. Open the file. When Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Open Reelo, then turn it on in Settings › Accessibility › Reelo.
  4. Xiaomi, POCO or Redmi: set Reelo's battery to No restrictions, turn on Autostart, and allow “Open new windows while running in the background”.

Play Protect may show a warning because Reelo isn't on the Play Store and uses Accessibility. That's expected for apps installed this way.
